# Career interests — the roles Maya is interested in

Maya's target roles sit where credit risk, pricing and applied AI meet in consumer lending. Her strength is turning models into measured business decisions, not model research. Longer term, she wants to lead a lending analytics function.

Level: Senior Manager or Associate Director, moving toward leading a lending analytics function within three years.

Roles she is not looking for: pure ML engineering, deep-learning research, or reporting-only roles.

## Target roles, in priority order

### 1. Credit strategy and decisioning
Focus of this target role (a role she wants, not work she has done): credit strategy across the lending lifecycle, from application decisions and limits to early collections.
Her relevant documented experience: the small-business PD scorecard at Harbourline that reduced 90-day arrears formation by 1.2%; the personal loan application scorecard rebuild at Northwind; and the collections prioritisation model.

### 2. Pricing and portfolio analytics
Focus of this target role (a role she wants, not work she has done): pricing and portfolio decisions tested against control groups.
Her relevant documented experience: £2.3M incremental margin for personal loans at 115% of target; 14 pricing experiments, 3 rolled out across the book.

### 3. Applied AI in lending
Focus of this target role (a role she wants, not work she has done): bringing ML and LLM tools into lending operations with the controls a regulated lender needs.
Her relevant documented experience: model governance and validation packs at Northwind, and her self-directed LLM work, which is outside her job.

### 4. Model risk and governance
Focus of this target role (a role she wants, not work she has done): validation, monitoring and documentation of lending models.
Her relevant documented experience: monthly monitoring for 9 production models; taking the mortgage retention model through model risk review; and her ACCA and audit background.

## Industries, settings and location

- Primary: banks and specialist lenders in personal, card and small-business lending.
- Also open to: fintech lenders and analytics consultancies.
- Location: based in Leeds, and open to roles across the UK and in Dublin and Amsterdam.

## What she wants the work to involve

- Owning outcomes measured against a control group, not counting models delivered.
- The full arc of a problem: framing the question, building the model or analysis, getting it adopted, and measuring what changed.
- AI applied with controls: human review, monitoring and clear accountability.
- Building capability: at Northwind she has hired and mentored a team of 3, and at Harbourline she trained 15+ collections agents.
- Senior stakeholder work: presenting to lending and risk committees.
