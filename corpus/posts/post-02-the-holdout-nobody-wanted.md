Nobody likes a holdout group. It's 5% of customers you deliberately don't help, and every campaign owner wants it gone.

I learned why it matters the hard way. Early in my audit career I signed off a controls test without checking that the sample was actually random. It wasn't: it skewed towards the branches that had prepared for us. A partner caught it in review. The conclusion I had written was confident and unsupported.

I think about that every time someone asks to shrink a control group. Without it, every result looks like a win, because you are comparing against nothing.

Our pricing tests kept a 5% holdout the whole way through. It was unpopular. It is also the only reason anyone believes the margin figure.
