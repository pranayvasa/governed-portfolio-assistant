Every lending team I talk to wants to automate something with AI this year. Very few can tell me how the process performs today.

That order matters. If you don't know the current approval rate, the current drop-off at each step, or how often a collections call actually changes an outcome, you can't tell whether automation helped. You can only tell that it's faster.

AI makes a measured process better and an unmeasured process fail faster. The failures just arrive at scale and look confident.

So my rule is boring: baseline first, with a control group where you can get one. Then automate one step. Then measure again.

It is the same discipline as a pricing test. Nobody would roll out a new rate card without a holdout. An AI step in the loan journey deserves the same treatment.
